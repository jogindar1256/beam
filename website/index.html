Send 100 GB from your PC to your Mac.

Beam moves files straight from one computer to the other over Wi-Fi, a hotspot or a cable. No cloud, no size limits, and if the connection drops, it picks up where it left off.

Made for really big transfers.

As fast as your network

Files go directly between the two computers, never through a server. On a cable you get the cable's full speed.

Survives dropped Wi-Fi

If the connection drops, Beam notices within seconds, reconnects on its own and continues from the last saved block.

Checked and encrypted

Every block is encrypted in transit and verified on arrival. Files that are already on the other computer are skipped.

Set up in two minutes.

  1. Install on both computers

    Download Beam on your PC and your Mac. Both need to be on the same Wi-Fi, a hotspot or a cable.

  2. Pick what to send

    The other computer appears on its own. Choose files or whole folders and press Send.

  3. Confirm the code once

    The first time, both screens show the same six digits. Confirm them and the computers trust each other from then on.

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Beam isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer asks for administrator permission so it can let Beam through Windows Firewall.

My Mac won't open Beam
  1. In Finder, open Applications, right-click Beam and choose Open, then Open again.
  2. If that doesn't work: System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway.
  3. If macOS says Beam is damaged, open Terminal and run xattr -cr /Applications/Beam.app, then open it again.
Do my files go through a server?

No. Files travel directly between your two computers, encrypted. Beam has no accounts and no cloud storage.

The other computer doesn't show up

Make sure both computers are on the same Wi-Fi network and Beam is open on both. Some networks in hotels and offices block devices from seeing each other; start a hotspot from the PC instead (in the app) or connect the computers with an Ethernet cable. You can also add the other computer by its IP address.

How do I get updates?

On Windows, Beam updates itself in the background and installs the update the next time it restarts, never during a transfer. On Mac, download the new version from this page.
